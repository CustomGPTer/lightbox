# Lightbox

Lightbox is a free Android gallery that cannot go online. It finds photos by the words and things in them and by where they were taken, edits them, tidies them and takes the hidden details out before you share. No adverts, no account.

Website: https://customgpter.github.io/lightbox/

Download: https://github.com/CustomGPTer/lightbox/releases/latest/download/Lightbox.apk
