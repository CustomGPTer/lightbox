What to know before relying on it

  • Lightbox can move, change and delete the photos and videos on your phone, because you ask it to. Deleted items wait in its bin for 30 days and are then gone for good.
  • Make safe changes the photo itself unless you ask for a copy. What is taken out cannot be got back.
  • The Safe pill means Lightbox read the file and found nothing beyond what your settings allow. It is not a guarantee that a photo cannot be traced by other means, and it does not hide what is visible in the picture.
  • Clean up makes suggestions. A photo it calls blurry or a look-alike may be one you want; look before you delete.
  • Search inside photos and the names of places are worked out by the app and will sometimes be wrong.
  • Keep backups of photos that matter to you. Lightbox is not a backup.
