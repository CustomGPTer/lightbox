Privacy

Lightbox collects nothing. It has no internet access, so it could not send anything if it tried.

What the app does with your photos

  • It reads the photos and videos on your phone to show them, to work out which are safe, and to find where each was taken.
  • If "Search inside photos" is on, it reads the words in each photo and works out what the photo shows. This is done on the phone by Google's ML Kit, which is built into the app. The parts of ML Kit that would normally report usage figures to Google are taken out of Lightbox, and with no internet permission nothing could be sent in any case.
  • It keeps a small database on the phone: favourites, tags, notes, stars, what each file was found to hold, what was read in each picture, and what is in the 30-day bin. Switching "Search inside photos" off deletes what was read.
  • It changes a photo only when you ask: Make safe, an edit saved in place of the original, tags written into the file, or a shot filed by the camera button.

All of that happens on the phone. Nothing is uploaded, and there are no accounts, adverts, analytics or crash reports.

Permissions

  • All files access. To show every album, and to move, clean, edit and delete photos in any folder.
  • Usage access (optional). To see when the camera app closes, so shots are filed then. Lightbox looks only at which app is on screen while a shoot is running; it keeps no record.
  • Notifications (optional). To show that a shoot is running, with Stop and Switch album buttons.
  • Fingerprint. Only if you switch the app lock on. Lightbox never sees your fingerprint; the phone answers yes or no.

Lightbox does not have the internet permission. You can see the full list on the phone: Settings, Apps, Lightbox, Permissions.

Sharing, and other apps choosing a photo

When you choose Share safe, or another app asks Lightbox for a photo with "Hand over a safe copy" on, a cleaned copy is made in the app's own scratch space and handed to that app. What that app does with it is up to that app. Scratch copies are cleared after a day.

The widget

The home-screen widget shows one of your photos to anyone who can see your home screen. With the app lock or the screenshot block on, it shows no photo.

This website
