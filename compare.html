A private gallery that also finds things

Fossify Gallery is a well-liked private gallery with no adverts. Lightbox shares that aim and adds search inside photos, places and clean-up. This page uses only what Fossify Gallery's own Google Play listing says.

What Lightbox does not do

Lightbox does not back up your photos, the map has no streets, and it does not group photos by face. It needs Android 13 or later on a 64-bit phone, and it is not on Google Play.

Where Fossify Gallery does more

Fossify Gallery is open source and on Google Play, so it updates itself. Its lock also offers a pattern.
