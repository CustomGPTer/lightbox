1.2 19 November 2026

First public release.

  • Photos by date and by album, a viewer with zoom and HDR, a video player, and a 30-day bin with Undo.
  • Search by name, album, month, tag, note, the words in a photo, what it shows and the town it was taken in.
  • Places: an offline map with pins, and photos listed by town.
  • The Safe pill, Make safe and Share safe, and a photo picker for other apps that can hand over a cleaned copy.
  • Editor: crop, turn, straighten, light and colour, filters, draw, blur, text. Video trim and mute.
  • Tags, notes and star ratings, written into JPEG files.
  • Clean up: duplicates, look-alikes, blurry photos, big videos, old screenshots.
  • Shoot into any album with the phone's own camera; shots can be cleaned and stamped on the way in.
  • Zip or PDF report of a selection; rename as a numbered set.
  • Backup file for favourites, tags and settings; home-screen photo widget; optional app lock.
  • No internet permission.

Lightbox cannot check for updates itself, because it has no internet access. Every version is on the GitHub releases page.

Lightbox is free. No adverts, no accounts, no paid version.
